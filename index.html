About me

Hi, I'm Yikang, a first-year graduate student of computational linguistics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I am currently advised by Hai Hu. Before that, I received my B.A. in English and Minor B.Eng. in CS from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).

Academically, I firstly learned about the linguistics from corpus/quantitative perspectives. After that, I learned about computational linguistics and natural language processing. This field appeals to me a lot. In future, I want to contribute to more linguistically-rich language intelligence and apply cutting-edge NLP techniques into studies of linguistics and digital humanities.

In daily life, I play the voilin since five, and I love classical music, especially symphonies of Mahler. To me, music is for emotion, Chinese and English for communication, and codes for computation. Anyway, they are all different forms of languages!

You can reach me via: yikangliu at sjtu dot edu dot cn
