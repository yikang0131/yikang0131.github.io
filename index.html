About me

Hi, I'm Yikang, a Ph.D. student in computational linguistics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I am currently advised by Rui Wang. My M.A. supervisors were Hai Hu and Rui Wang. Before that, I received my M.A. in linguistics from SJTU, and a B.A. in English and a minor B.Eng. in CS from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).

For now, I am trying to investigate properties of human language and human language cognition by building and analyzing language models, or neural networks in general.

For my daily hobbies, I like to play the violin and listen to classical music, especially symphonies and violin concertos. I was once a violin player in the student orchestra of HUST, performing at a New Year Concert in the magnificent Qintai Concert Hall in Wuhan!

Selected Papers

A Systematic Assessment of Language Models with Linguistic Minimal Pairs in Chinese

Yikang Liu, Yeting Shen, Hongao Zhu, Lilong Xu, Zhiheng Qian, Siyuan Song, Kejia Zhang, Jialong Tang, Pei Zhang, Baosong Yang, Rui Wang, Hai Hu

ZhoBLiMP evaluates Chinese grammar with 35,000 minimal pairs across 118 paradigms. Length-adjusted scoring reduces bias from unequal sentence lengths, while anaphora, quantifiers, and ellipsis remain difficult for models up to 32B parameters.

Transactions of ACL · 2026
Paper Code

Translationese-index: Using likelihood ratios for graded and generalizable measurement of translationese

Yikang Liu, Wanyang Zhang, Yiming Wang, Jialong Tang, Pei Zhang, Baosong Yang, Fei Huang, Rui Wang, Hai Hu

The T-index measures how strongly a translation exhibits translationese using the likelihood ratio from two contrastively fine-tuned language models. The score aligns with human judgments and generalizes to unseen genres, authors, and language pairs.

EMNLP · 2025
Paper Code

MELA: Multilingual evaluation of linguistic acceptability

Ziyin Zhang*, Yikang Liu*, Weifang Huang, Junyu Mao, Rui Wang, Hai Hu (*Equal contribution)

MELA benchmarks linguistic acceptability with 46,000 examples across 10 languages. In cross-lingual experiments, XLM-R fine-tuned on 500 Icelandic examples reached about 0.23 MCC on Chinese acceptability judgments.

ACL · 2024
Paper Data

ArguGPT: Evaluating, understanding and identifying argumentative essays generated by GPT models

Yikang Liu, Ziyin Zhang, Wanyang Zhang, Shisen Yue, Xiaojing Zhao, Xinyuan Cheng, Yiwen Zhang, Hai Hu

ArguGPT pairs 4,038 GPT-generated argumentative essays with 4,115 human-written essays matched by prompt. In the study, fine-tuned RoBERTa exceeded 90% accuracy at both essay and sentence levels.
